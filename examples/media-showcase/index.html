Media: a pipeline you click through

This card is the kit's example for baked media. One row travels stage 0 → 1 → 5: click the picture and it moves, carrying what it holds at that moment. All numbers are made up, so every picture carries the “illustrative” badge.

Five clicks, not a video: the picture stops where you stopped, and the line under it says what the click changed. Anything the picture carries — a category, a row id, a split — is text you can read, select and search.

Two thirds of the input is gone after filter. That asymmetry is the reason the card exists.

The long tail is what normalization is for: running shoes, Running Shoes and running shoe are three bars until canon_label folds them into one.
